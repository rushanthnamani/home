Every idea needs its colours.

Varnika designs illustrations, patterns and brand identities for people who want to look like nobody else.

See the work Say hello

The name means "colour".

Varnika comes from varna, the old word for colour. It is how the studio works: start with a palette, then let shape and pattern follow.

Projects range from book covers and packaging to textile prints and murals. Each one is drawn by hand first, then refined on screen.
